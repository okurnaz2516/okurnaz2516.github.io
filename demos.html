Speech demos

Small, hands-on demos of the technology I work on. Record with your microphone or upload a file; audio is sent to a Hugging Face Space for processing and is not stored.

An ECAPA-TDNN speaker embedding is extracted from each recording, and the cosine similarity between the two embeddings is compared with a decision threshold. This is the "ASV" half of the spoofing-aware speaker verification systems I study.

1 · Enrollment recording

A few seconds of the claimed speaker (5–15 s works well).

2 · Test recording

A different utterance — same person or someone else.

Any-to-any voice conversion with kNN-VC: each WavLM frame of the source speech is replaced by its nearest neighbours from the target speaker's recording, then vocoded. The content comes from the source, the voice from the target. Longer target recordings give better results.

1 · Source speech (what is said)

Record yourself saying something (up to 30 s).

2 · Target voice (who says it)

A recording of the target voice — 30 s to 2 min is ideal.

Zero-shot voice-cloning text-to-speech with XTTS-v2: a short reference recording conditions the model, which then speaks your text in that voice. Supports Turkish, English and 15 other languages.

2 · Reference voice
